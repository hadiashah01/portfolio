# Copywriting Direction

## Tone Rules
- Direct, confident, humble.
- No jargon, no "ninja/guru," no "passionate about technology."
- No AI / LLM / vibe coder mentions anywhere.
- No student / learner framing.
- Speak like a person who builds websites for businesses.

## Section-by-Section Copy

### Hero
**Headline:** Thoughtful websites. Carefully built.
**Subhead:** I'm a frontend developer building responsive web experiences for businesses and digital products — with visual design, useful functionality, and clear communication.
**CTA:** Discuss a project / Contact me

### Work
**Section intro:** A selection of websites I've built.
**Per project:**
- Title
- One-line outcome (e.g., "A business website for a local consultancy.")
- Short rationale (1–2 sentences): why it was built this way.
- Link: Live site / Repo (optional)

### Why Me
**Section intro:** Why work with me.
**Bullets (honest, not exaggerated):**
- I focus on frontend work for business websites, not generalist full-stack promises.
- I build in React and Next.js so sites are fast and maintainable.
- I communicate clearly and deliver what I say I will.
- I don't oversell AI or automation; I build well-crafted websites.

### About
**Who I am:** Frontend developer. I build websites for businesses and digital products.
**What I build:** Responsive web experiences with clear communication and useful functionality.
**What I don't do:** I'm not a design agency, not a full-stack shop, and not an AI automation service.
**Learning (supporting only):** Brief note on React/Next.js background, placed here not in the hero.

### Contact
**CTA:** Have a website project? Let's talk.
**Fields:** Name, Email, Message (keep it short).
**Footer:** Email address + location (optional) + short credit.

## What to Remove from Current Draft
- "Current Focus" card (replaces with project evidence).
- "Skills Overview" badge list (moves into project context and About).
- "Learning Journey" timeline (moves to About footer area).
- Repetitive information cards.
