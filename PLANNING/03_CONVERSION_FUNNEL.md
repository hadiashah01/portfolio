# Conversion Funnel

## Goal
Turn a visitor into a lead: someone who clicks contact and inquires about a website project.

## Funnel Steps

### 1. Arrival (Hero)
- Visitor lands and immediately understands who you are and what you build.
- **Decision point:** Scroll down or click Contact.
- **Design:** Value prop + primary CTA above the fold. No carousel, no video.

### 2. Proof (Work)
- Visitor sees real projects, not skill badges.
- Each project answers: What was it? What did you do? What was the result?
- **Decision point:** Click a project or continue to "Why Me."

### 3. Trust (Why Me + About)
- Visitor answers: Why hire this person? Can they deliver? Are they honest?
- Direct, non-exaggerated answers about business focus and frontend ownership.
- **Decision point:** Click Contact.

### 4. Action (Contact)
- Low-friction form + email.
- **Decision point:** Submit.

## Anti-Patterns to Exclude
- Hero animations that delay the value proposition.
- "Learning journey" or "current focus" taking up prime real estate.
- Generic "hire me" language that sounds like a template.
- Contact hidden behind a menu or multiple clicks.

## Success Metric
A visitor can answer these questions within 30 seconds:
- Who is this?
- What do they build?
- Have they done work like mine?
- How do I contact them?
- Why should I hire them over someone else?
