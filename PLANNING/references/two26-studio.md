# Reference: Two26 Studio (two26.studio)

## What Works for Them
- **Distinctive project presentation:** Projects are cases, not grid thumbnails.
- **Visual prominence of work:** Imagery and layout make the project the hero of its page.
- **Playful creative direction:** Memorable without sacrificing clarity of the work itself.

## What We Can Utilize (Adapted)
- Project detail format: context/challenge → your approach → outcome.
- Prominent project previews (screenshots or live links) with concise copy.
- Make each project feel like a real piece of work, not a generic card.

## What to Avoid
- Playfulness that undermines business credibility (you are a frontend developer for businesses, not a creative studio).
- Excessive motion or novelty interactions that distract from the work.
