# Reference: Nesil Noble (nesilnoble.com)

## What Works for Them
- **Personal narrative:** Strong personal voice; the visitor feels like they're meeting a person.
- **Story-first presentation:** Work is framed inside a larger story about who the creator is.
- **Immersive but readable:** The presentation draws you in without hiding the content.

## What We Can Utilize (Adapted)
- A personal "why" in the hero or About: why you build websites, what kind of clients you like working with.
- A narrative thread connecting your projects (e.g., "I focus on small business sites that need to look credible and load fast").
- A warm, human tone in copy so the site doesn't feel like a template.

## What to Avoid
- Immersive effects that obscure content or slow down the initial load.
- Hero animations that delay the value proposition.
