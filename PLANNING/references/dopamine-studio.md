# Reference: Dopamine Studio (dopaminestudio.io)

## What Works for Them
- **Explains the thinking behind the work:** Shows rationale, not just final results.
- **Process transparency:** Answers "why was it built this way?"
- **Decision-focused:** Each project page communicates intent.

## What We Can Utilize (Adapted)
- A short "why I built it this way" line under each project (one or two sentences).
- Focus on decisions: why Next.js, why this layout, why this color — only where it matters to the client.
- Keep it concise; a project story, not a long-form case study.

## What to Avoid
- Long-form case studies that become bloated for a small portfolio.
- Over-explaining simple technical decisions that the client doesn't care about.
