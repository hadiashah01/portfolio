# Reference: Pamidor Design (pamidordesign.co)

## What Works for Them
- **Editorial art direction:** Strong, confident typography establishes identity immediately.
- **Clear point of view:** The site says who they are before showing what they do.
- **Curated, not exhaustive:** Selected projects only; quality over quantity.
- **Whitespace as design element:** Generous spacing gives each project room to breathe.
- **Separation of creative space:** Main site is client-facing; experimental work lives apart so it doesn't dilute the core message.

## What We Can Utilize (Adapted)
- Typographic hierarchy as the primary visual system (one or two typefaces max).
- Lead with identity: hero states clearly who you are and what you build.
- Curate the project list. Show only your best business websites.
- Use whitespace to separate sections and guide the eye, not decoration.

## What to Avoid
- Overly experimental layouts that hurt readability or scanability.
- Splitting the portfolio into a "creative space" separate from the main site — that confuses the conversion goal for a solo developer.
- Treating the portfolio like an art gallery; it is a business tool first.
