# Reference: Seb Graf (sebg.ch)

## What Works for Them
- **Guided portfolio storytelling:** Introduces self, then walks the visitor through projects.
- **Explains contribution:** Each project answers "what did I actually do?" not just "what does it look like?"
- **Contact is easy to find:** Persistent or well-placed CTA; no hunting.
- **Personality through narrative:** The voice carries the site, not decorative motion.

## What We Can Utilize (Adapted)
- Project stories framed around your contribution: context, your role, outcome.
- A clear narrative thread from hero → work → contact.
- Contact accessibility: a CTA in the hero and a contact section at the bottom.
- Keep the "personality" in the copy and project rationale, not in UI tricks.

## What to Avoid
- Interactive personality that becomes decorative noise (hover gimmicks, cursor effects).
- Complex navigation or non-standard interactions that slow the visitor down.
