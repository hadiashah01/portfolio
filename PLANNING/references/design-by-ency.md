# Reference: Design by Ency (designbyency.com)

## What Works for Them
- **Clear positioning:** The value proposition is immediate and unambiguous.
- **Contact conversion:** Low-friction CTA; the site wants you to get in touch.
- **Direct messaging:** No fluff, no generic studio language.

## What We Can Utilize (Adapted)
- One-sentence value prop in the hero.
- A direct, simple contact mechanism (email + short form).
- CTA placement: primary CTA in hero, secondary CTA at end of work section, email in footer.

## What to Avoid
- Generic agency language that doesn't sound like an individual developer (e.g., "full-service digital partner").
- Hiding the contact CTA behind multiple clicks.
