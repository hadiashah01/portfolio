# UI/UX Direction

## Visual Identity
- **Editorial digital-craft:** Typography-first, deliberate composition.
- **Canvas:** Warm neutral background (off-white / warm gray), not stark white.
- **Accent:** One restrained accent color for CTAs and key emphasis only.
- **Imagery:** Strong project screenshots or previews; no stock hero illustrations.

## Layout
- **Single-column reading flow** with max-width content (~720–800px) for easy scanning.
- **Generous whitespace** between sections; whitespace separates, never decorates.
- **Project grid:** 2–3 columns on desktop, 1 column on mobile. Prominent previews, concise text.

## Navigation & Structure
- **One-page scroll:** Hero → Work → Why Me → About → Contact.
- **Anchor nav:** Work, About, Contact only.
- **Contact accessibility:** Primary CTA in hero; sticky bottom CTA on mobile; contact section + email in footer.

## Motion & Interaction
- **Functional only:** Smooth scroll, subtle hover states on links/buttons.
- **No award-site animations:** No parallax, no reveal-on-scroll sequences, no auto-playing video, no cursor effects.
- **Performance first:** Fast load, no heavy JS libraries for decoration.

## Accessibility & Usability
- Clear heading hierarchy (H1 → H2 → H3).
- Sufficient contrast for body text and accent buttons.
- Form labels and focus states on contact form.
- Mobile-first responsive breakpoints.

## Device Behavior
- **Desktop:** Max-width content, side anchor nav optional, hover states.
- **Mobile:** Stacked sections, sticky contact CTA at bottom, touch-friendly tap targets.
